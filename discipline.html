Rules and Regulations

Procedure for Disciplinary Cases

1. A violation report is filed by the reporting officer or faculty member upon witnessing or receiving a complaint.

2. The concerned student is notified and given the opportunity to explain their side within three school days.

3. Minor offenses are addressed through a verbal warning or written reprimand.

4. Major or repeated offenses are elevated to the Discipline Committee.

Implementation of Disciplinary Sanctions

Sanctions range from a verbal warning to suspension or voiding of institutional scholarships, depending on severity and frequency.

Student Categories

Students are categorized as First Offense, Repeated Offense, or Habitual Offender.
